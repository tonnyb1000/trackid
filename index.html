Privacy & How This Works

  • Explicit consent: Only your own device is tracked, and only while tracking is active.
  • Session memory only: Coordinates and your session path stay in this tab’s memory. Nothing is saved to disk or sent to a backend server.
  • Map tiles & network: Loading map tiles (Esri World Street Map / OpenStreetMap) requires internet access and reveals your IP address to the tile provider.
  • Stopping & clearing: Stop tracking stops all new updates while keeping your last marker visible. Clear path removes the drawn session trail.
  • Accuracy & background limits: Browsers estimate location using GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell towers; updates may pause if your phone locks or the browser goes to the background.
